Answers before you start your trial

What happens to emergency calls?

RingVane separates emergency calls from routine ones using the rules you set, and sends a phone alert for each emergency.

Does RingVane just take messages?

No. It books the job into your schedule and logs the call with an AI summary, transcript and audio.

How will I know a job was booked?

You get a phone alert for each booking, and the job appears on your schedule. The caller gets a text confirmation.

Do I need to change my phone number or carrier?

No. You keep your current number and carrier. You forward your business line to RingVane, and you can switch forwarding off at any time.

What happens after the 14-day trial?

The trial ends after 14 days and you choose whether to continue at $199 per month. No credit card is required to start, so you are never charged without your approval.
